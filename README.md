## About

I’m **Niyomugabo Frederic**, a full stack developer with 5+ years of experience building websites, mobile applications, APIs, dashboards, and business platforms.

At [LIGHTMGROUP](https://lightmgroup.com), I stay involved throughout delivery: understanding the workflow, designing the experience, building the backend, integrating external services, deploying the system, and supporting it in production.
